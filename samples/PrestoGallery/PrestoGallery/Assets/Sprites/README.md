# Sprites

Characters from Kenney's [Platformer Characters](https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-characters) pack, dedicated to the
public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The sheets are the pack's own
tilesheets (9 × 3 cells of 80 × 110 px, 24 poses); the sequence is eight of its separate pose images, renamed in order.
